# etexts — transcriptions made by adhyayana-sahakārī

Five commentaries that we transcribed ourselves from scans of printed editions, as shown on
[adhyayana-sahakārī](https://constrainedrandomvar.github.io/adhyayana-sahakari/). Licence: **CC BY 4.0** for our
transcription, editing, notes and apparatus. Please credit "adhyayana-sahakārī". The printed works are old and, as far as we know, in
the public domain in India. Each section below gives what we know about copyright.

Each text has two files:
- **`<name>.json`** — `_meta` (work, edition, scan, scope, field list) followed by one entry per unit, keyed by **our** reference
  (the numbering of the base text on our site). Where the edition numbers differ, its number is given as `edRef`.
- **`<name>.txt`** — a plain reading copy of the same text. Each unit starts with a `== ref ==` line.

The Devanāgarī follows the edited text exactly. The print's orthography is kept: anusvāra, avagraha and sandhi spacing are as printed.
We have not standardised spelling. Common conventions:
- **⚑ / `flags`** — a note on a word, given as `word` (+ `occurrence` = the nth time that word appears in the unit) and `note`.
  These cover misprints in the print (shown as printed), manuscript variants and supplied readings.
- **⸮** (Vārttikasāra only) — the reading after it is doubtful. It is kept as read.
- **`corrections`** (Brahmatattvaprakāśikā only) — printer's misprints that we corrected. `printed` keeps the original reading,
  `basis` gives the reason, and `uncertain: true` means we made the change but are not sure of it.

**Fidelity check.** A script (`verify_etexts.py`, in our working repository) compares every file, letter by letter, with what the site shows. All five are
identical letter for letter (the Saddarśanam commentary since 2026-10-25, when two model-made changes on the site were removed). The site's
pada-split copies and editorial citation links are left out of the comparison. Including daṇḍas and digits, two further kinds of
difference come from the site's display layer, not from these files:
- **Brahmatattvaprakāśikā:** the site corrects 4 printed citation numbers whose quotation is found at a different place. These files
  keep the numbers as printed:
  - 1.3.31: छा. 3.11.1 → 3.19.1
  - 3.2.13: क. 4.11 → 4.10
  - 4.1.13: मु. 2.2.8 → 2.2.9
  - 4.4.20: क. 2.5.15 → 2.2.15
- **The two prakaraṇa commentaries (4–5):** the site shows each one split into sentences by our gloss model, which sometimes adds
  a daṇḍa. These files keep the punctuation of the proofread transcription.

Sources, scripts and checks: the files are generated by `work/share-tamal/export/export_etexts.py` in our working repository
(not public), from its commit 4186f707 (2026-10-25), checked against the academy site build f9962b0. Every check was done by people and machines, as described per text. **Gemini** (a Google model) was used only to
*suggest* or *flag* readings. Every suggestion was settled against the scan before it was applied, and the model never
standardised the print.

## 1. Brahmatattvaprakāśikā — `brahmatattvaprakashika/`
- **Work:** ब्रह्मतत्त्वप्रकाशिका, a vṛtti on the Brahmasūtras by Sadāśivendra Sarasvatī.
- **Base edition:** Trivandrum Sanskrit Series 7, ed. T. Ganapati Sastri, Trivandrum 1909 (from MSS क and ख). Scan: archive.org
  `brahma-tattva-prakasika-of-sadasivendra-sarasvati-ganpati-sastry-t.-trivandrum-sanskrit-series-7`.
- **Witness:** Sri Vani Vilas Press, Srirangam, 1909 — archive.org `BrahmaSutraVrittiSadashivendra` (600 dpi).
- **Method:**
  - Both editions were OCR'd with Google Cloud Vision, parsed sūtra by sūtra and aligned word by word.
  - Every difference between the two editions was settled on the TSS scan.
  - We also ran a ligature sweep, a citation-digit sweep, and a Gemini page check (flags only; each flag decided on the scan).
  - The printer's errata (शुद्धिपत्रम्) are applied. A "conservation" check confirms that no text was lost or added in parsing.
- **Contents:** 555 sūtras plus the opening (`intro`: maṅgala verses and lead-in). Each sūtra gives:
  - the printed adhikaraṇa heading
  - `avatarika`, the vṛtti's lead-in printed before the sūtra
  - the sūtra as printed in TSS, with its printed number
  - the `vritti`
  - TSS scan pages.

  There are **86 misprint corrections** (12 marked uncertain) and **274 flags**: MS variants placed in the text, pāṭhabheda-table
  readings, supplied readings and errata.
- **Apparatus** (`apparatus/`):
  - `editor-footnotes.json` — the editor's 131 footnotes (MS variants), most anchored to a scan line.
  - `pathabheda-table.json` — the printed पाठभेदः table (88 rows, क vs ख).
  - `vani-vilas-variants.json` — 891 genuine Vani Vilas readings that differ from TSS.
  - `supplied-readings.json` — readings supplied from Vani Vilas where the TSS print is covered by a library stamp or uninked type.
- **Copyright:** the 1909 prints are public domain.

## 2. Śaṅkarānandī — `shankaranandi/`
- **Work:** गीतातात्पर्यबोधिनी (Śaṅkarānandī), Śaṅkarānanda's commentary on the Bhagavadgītā. As far as we know, there was no
  e-text of it before this one.
- **Base edition:** Nirṇayasāgara Press, Bombay, 1916 (Sanskrit only). Scan: archive.org
  `BhagavataGitaWithShankaranandiTika1916NirnayaSagarPress` (eGangotri; B. N. Kalla collection; marked CC0; 600 ppi).
- **Witness:** the Bhole Baba edition with Hindi — archive.org `shankaranandi-gita.bhasya-hindi`. Its Sanskrit was used only to
  check our reading of the base, and no text was taken from it.
- **Method:**
  - Both editions were OCR'd with Vision and collated note by note.
  - At each of the 5,971 disagreements, Gemini read a crop of the base scan; where that read was unclear, a second blind read
    followed.
  - The base print decides every reading: 1,512 corrections, the doubtful ones settled by eye on the crops.
  - A former owner's pen marks, running heads and signature marks were removed.
- **Contents:**
  - 687 note units, keyed by the verse they comment on. `group` links verses that are commented on together.
  - `scan_leaves` gives the archive.org leaf ids. `parts` are pratīka or vyākhyā; `printed_pratika` marks the 607 pratīkas
    printed in bold.
  - 24 ⚑ `flags` mark misprints in the print, shown as printed.
  - `colophons.json` holds the colophons, chapter titles, speaker lines and signature marks (kept out of the notes).
- **Copyright:** the 1916 print is public domain. The scan is marked CC0.

## 3. Bṛhadāraṇyaka-vārttikasāra with the Laghusaṅgrahā — `vartikasara/`
- **Work:** बृहदारण्यकवार्तिकसारः, Vidyāraṇya's digest of Sureśvara's Bṛhadāraṇyaka-vārttika, with Maheśvaratīrtha's
  लघुसङ्ग्रहा ṭīkā.
- **Base edition:** Chowkhamba Sanskrit Series 205, Benares 1915 (issued in parts; ed. Bhāu Śāstrī Vajhe). Scan: archive.org
  `brhadaranyakavar00madh` (University of Toronto, Robarts Library). As far as we know it is the only digital copy.
- **Witness:** a second physical copy — archive.org `Brihadaranyaka_Vartikasarah_-_Vidyaranya_Swami`.
- **Method:**
  - Vision OCR of both scans.
  - Gemini made text-only suggestions (13,125). These were checked against the second copy, then decided on crops of the base
    scan: a first pass, plus a 300 dpi zoom pass for the hard cases.
  - The publisher's errata are applied (29 of 36; 2 were already correct).
  - Readings that could not be settled are kept and marked **⸮** (329).
- **Contents:**
  - 5,035 verses, keyed by the Kāṇva Bṛhadāraṇyaka mantra they follow (159 mantras), plus the sāra's introduction (`intro`).
  - 3,931 Laghusaṅgrahā notes, each joined to its verse.
  - Each verse keeps its half-lines and printed numbers as printed, with `pdf_page` of the base scan. `va` marks the 181 lines the
    print marks वा०.
  - Scope: introduction and 1.1–4.6, which is all the sāra covers.
- **Copyright:** the 1915 print is public domain.

## 4. Ātmabodha with the Vedāntasāra ṭīkā — `atmabodha-padmapada/`
- **Work:** वेदान्तसार, the ṭīkā on Śaṅkara's Ātmabodha ascribed to Padmapāda.
- **Edition:** *Prakaraṇadvādaśī* (प्रकरणद्वादशी), Shri Shankarabhagavatpada's twelve prakaraṇas with the commentaries of
  Padmapāda, Vidyāraṇya, Ānandagiri and others; edited by S. Subrahmanya Shastri; Advaita Grantha Ratna Manjusha, Ratna 20;
  Mahesh Research Institute, published by Shri Dakshinamurti Matha Prakashan, Varanasi; second edition, 2004 (Vikrama 2061).
  The Ātmabodha with Padmapāda's ṭīkā is item 6, printed pp. ४८९–५०२. Identified by matching our scan page for page against
  the full volume (e.g. p. ४९५: verses 29–33 and the ṭīkā on 28–33, identical line for line).
- **Source scan:** a scan of pp. ४८९–५०२ of that volume.
- **Method:**
  - Vision OCR, with rows rebuilt from the word geometry: mūla block above, ṭīkā below the rule.
  - Gemini made 211 suggestions while looking at the page image. 154 were accepted after a word-crop check; its
    normalisations were rejected.
  - The printer's errors are kept as printed.
- **Contents:**
  - The ṭīkā on 67 verses, keyed by our Ātmabodha numbering, plus the heading, maṅgala and colophon.
  - The edition numbers differently: it lacks AB 8, swaps 36 and 37, and prints Gītā 15.6 as its v61, whose one-line ṭīkā is
    appended to AB 61. `edRef` gives the edition's number where it differs.
  - The mūla is not included; our site's Ātmabodha mūla comes from a different source.
- **Copyright:** the ṭīkā itself is an old work. The volume is distributed by the Mahesh Research Institute as a free download; the
  2004 edition states "All rights reserved by the Publisher". Our transcription is shared with credit to the publisher.

## 5. Saddarśanam with Kapālī Śāstrī's bhāṣya — `saddarshanam-kapali/`
- **Work:** सद्दर्शनभाष्यम्, the commentary by Bhāradvāja Kapālī (T. V. Kapali Sastry, 1886–1953) on Kāvyakaṇṭha Gaṇapati Muni's
  Saddarśanam, the Sanskrit rendering of Ramaṇa Maharṣi's Tamil *Uḷḷadu Nāṟpadu*.
- **Edition:** the title page reads «श्रीभगवद्रमणमहर्षिविरचितद्राविडग्रन्थस्य संस्कृतानुवादात्मकम् सद्दर्शनम्
  श्रीवासिष्ठगणपतिमुनिविरचितम् भारद्वाजेन कपालिना विरचितेन भाष्येण संयुतम् अरुणाचलक्षेत्रस्थ-रमणाश्रमकार्यनिर्वाहकेन
  श्रीनिरञ्जनानन्दस्वामिना प्रकाशितम्» — "(First Published in 1931)". It was published by Sri Niranjanananda Swami, Sri
  Ramanasramam, Tiruvannamalai. The year of this printing is not stated.
- **Source scan:** archive.org `saddarshanamwithsanskritcommentarybykapalisastryt.v._202004_957_J` (Digital Library of India).
- **Method:**
  - Vision OCR, segmented verse by verse, then proofread in full by hand.
  - 139 further corrections came from a model's detect-only pass. Each was accepted per verse after review, never as a global
    replace.
- **Contents:** the commentary on all 44 units: prārthanā 0.1–0.2, verses 1–40, closing verses 41–42. Each unit has the lead-in
  (`avatarika`) and the commentary, with paragraph breaks kept as blank lines. The bhūmikā (pp. 10–37) and the mūla are not
  included.
- **Checked against the scan:** 0.1 reads हृद्देशस्य (the OCR had dropped the द्द conjunct; corrected from p. 41). The site's
  reading view now shows exactly this text (two places where our gloss model had altered it were fixed on 2026-10-25).
- **Copyright:** Kapali Sastry died on 17 August 1953 (Wikipedia), so in India his commentary entered the public domain in 2014
  (life + 60 years). The scan is marked CC0 on archive.org.
